# Growth Strategy Analysis

Analyze a product's growth strategy using frameworks from Lenny's Podcast growth experts including Sarah Tavel, Casey Winters, Brian Balfour, and Brian Chesky.

## Instructions

When the user describes their product and growth challenge, analyze it through these frameworks:

### 1. Sarah Tavel's Hierarchy of Engagement
Evaluate the product across three levels:

**Level 1: Growing Engaged Users (Core Action)**
- What is the core action of this product?
- Is the product optimizing for this action or vanity metrics (downloads, sign-ups, MAU)?
- Does the new user experience lead users to complete the core action?
- What % of new users complete the core action in their first session/week?

**Level 2: Retaining Engaged Users (Accruing Benefits)**
- As users engage more, do they accumulate value that makes the product better for them?
- Examples: social graph (Facebook), saved content (Pinterest), history/preferences (Spotify)
- Is there a flywheel where more engagement → more personalized → more engagement?

**Level 3: Self-Perpetuating (Mounting Losses)**
- What would users lose if they left?
- Is switching cost increasing over time?
- Are there network effects that compound?

### 2. Casey Winters' Kindle vs. Fire Framework
- **Kindle strategies**: Non-scalable hacks that exist only to unlock fire strategies
  - Manual outreach, personal invitations, hand-curated content
  - These validate the model but can't scale
- **Fire strategies**: Scalable loops that drive millions of users
  - SEO, viral loops, content loops, paid acquisition at positive ROI
  - These must be the eventual growth engine

What kindle strategies are needed now, and what fire strategies will they unlock?

### 3. Brian Chesky's Growth Philosophy
- Are you relying on performance marketing (lasers) when you need brand marketing (chandeliers)?
- If you built the best possible product and told people about it, would growth happen?
- "If you build a great product and no one knows about it, did you even build a product?"
- Is your marketing team educating people on unique benefits, or just running ads?

### 4. Product-Market Fit Signals
From multiple guests, assess:
- **Retention curve**: Does it flatten or go to zero?
- **Organic growth**: Are users telling others without incentives?
- **Pull from market**: Are users asking for more, or do you need to push?
- **Usage frequency**: Does it match the expected cadence for this category?

### Output Format

1. Current state assessment across each framework
2. Top 3 growth opportunities ranked by expected impact
3. Recommended kindle → fire strategy sequence
4. Key metrics to track (and which vanity metrics to stop tracking)
5. Risks and pre-mortem on the growth strategy
