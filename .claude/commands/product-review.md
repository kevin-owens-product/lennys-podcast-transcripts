# Product Review Framework

Run a structured product review using frameworks from Lenny's Podcast guests. Analyze a product idea, feature, or strategy using multiple lenses.

## Instructions

When the user describes a product, feature, or strategy, evaluate it through these frameworks:

### 1. Brian Chesky's Coherence Test
- Does this feel like it was made by 10 people or 1,000?
- Can you tell a clear story about it? If you can't explain it simply, the product isn't ready.
- Does it connect to the broader product narrative/roadmap?
- Is engineering interconnected with how users will learn about this?

### 2. Shreyas Doshi's Three Levels Check
- **Impact level**: What is the measurable outcome for customers and the business?
- **Execution level**: What are the dependencies, risks, and timeline?
- **Optics level**: How will stakeholders and users become aware of this?
- Which level are you defaulting to? Are you aligned with leadership?

### 3. Teresa Torres's Opportunity Solution Tree
- What **outcome** does this serve?
- What **opportunity** (unmet need/pain/desire) does this address?
- What **assumption** are you making that, if wrong, would invalidate this?
- Have you explored at least 3 different solutions to this opportunity?

### 4. Tobi Lutke's First Principles Check
- What path dependence is baked into this approach?
- What was true when the prior solution was built that may no longer be true?
- If you re-derived this from scratch with today's building blocks, would you arrive at the same design?
- Is this "a good version of what everyone else does" or genuinely differentiated?

### 5. Add a Zero (Brian Chesky)
- What would it take to make this 10X better or serve 10X more people?
- What breaks at 10X that reveals a flawed assumption?
- Are you running the ball or throwing a pass?

### Output Format

Provide the analysis as a structured review with clear recommendations, risks, and a suggested pre-mortem (top 3 tigers, paper tigers, and elephants).
