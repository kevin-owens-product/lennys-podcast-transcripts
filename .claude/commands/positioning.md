# Product Positioning Workshop

Guide a product positioning exercise using April Dunford's framework from her Lenny's Podcast episodes and her books Obviously Awesome and Sales Pitch.

## Instructions

When the user describes their product, guide them through a positioning exercise:

### Step 1: Identify Real Alternatives
- What would your best customers do if your product didn't exist?
- Don't just list competitors—include "do nothing," spreadsheets, manual processes, hiring someone
- This is NOT your competitive set; it's your customer's real alternative set

### Step 2: Identify Differentiated Capabilities
- What can you do that the alternatives cannot?
- Focus on capabilities, not features
- These must be things you can actually prove/demonstrate

### Step 3: Map Capabilities to Value
- For each differentiated capability, what value does it enable for the customer?
- Value must be expressed in terms customers care about (revenue, time saved, risk reduced)
- Separate the effort to build something from the effort to talk about it (John Collison insight)

### Step 4: Identify Best-Fit Customers
- Who cares most about the value you deliver?
- What characteristics do your best customers share?
- What "current" or market dynamic pulls them toward your solution? (Sarah Tavel: think currents, not bodies of water)

### Step 5: Define Your Market Category
- What market frame makes your strengths obvious?
- Does the category help or hurt you?
- Consider: existing category, subcategory, or new category creation

### Step 6: Build the Sales Pitch
From April Dunford's Sales Pitch framework:
1. **Setup**: Define the market context and the key change/insight
2. **Problem**: What's wrong with the status quo given this change?
3. **Solution approach**: What should the ideal solution look like?
4. **Your differentiated value**: Here's what we uniquely deliver
5. **Proof**: Evidence that your value claims are real
6. **Close**: Make it easy to take the next step

Key insight: "40-60% of B2B purchases end in no decision—not because alternatives are better, but because buyers can't choose confidently. Your pitch must make choosing easy."

### Output Format

1. Positioning statement (one paragraph)
2. Value proposition mapped to differentiated capabilities
3. Ideal customer profile
4. Sales pitch narrative outline
5. Objection handling for top 3 likely objections
